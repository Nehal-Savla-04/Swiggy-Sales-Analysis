 🍔 Swiggy Sales Analysis | Excel Dashboard

 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Swiggy Sales Analysis Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel**. It analyzes food-ordering sales performance across different cities, states, food types and time periods.
The dashboard uses Excel PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers and KPI cards to transform transactional data into meaningful business insights.

 1. Problem Statement

Food delivery platforms generate large volumes of transactional data across multiple restaurants, locations and food categories. Analyzing this data is essential for understanding sales performance, customer 
preferences and ordering patterns.
The objective of this project is to build an interactive Excel dashboard that answers the following business questions:
* What are the total sales, total orders and average order value?
* How does sales performance vary across months, weeks and days?
* Which cities and states contribute to overall revenue?
* What is the sales distribution between vegetarian and non-vegetarian food?
* How does quarterly sales performance compare?
* What are the overall customer rating metrics?

 2. About the Dataset

The dataset contains Swiggy food-ordering records used to analyze sales, restaurant performance, geographical distribution and customer ratings.

Key analytical dimensions:
* Order and sales information
* Restaurant names
* Food categories and food types
* Cities and states
* Order dates
* Customer ratings
Additional columns were created in Excel to support time-based analysis and PivotTable reporting.

The workbook is organized into three worksheets:
| Worksheet | Description                                             |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Data      | Source dataset with additional columns for analysis     |
| Analysis  | PivotTables used to summarize data and generate charts  |
| Dashboard | Interactive visual report with KPIs, charts and slicers |
Tools: Microsoft Excel, PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers and Excel formulas.

The dashboard highlights five key metrics to provide a quick overview of business performance.

| KPI                 |   Value |

* Total Sales: Total revenue generated from food orders.

 4. Dashboard and Visualizations

i. Monthly Sales Trend
A line chart displays monthly sales performance from January through August. It helps identify revenue fluctuations, higher-sales months and periods of declining sales.

ii. Daily Sales Trend
A column chart compares sales across the seven days of the week, making it easier to identify daily ordering patterns and high-revenue days.

iii. Weekly Sales Trend
A column chart presents sales across 36 reporting weeks, providing a more detailed view of revenue fluctuations over time.

iv. Sales by Food Type
A doughnut chart compares the revenue contribution of vegetarian and non-vegetarian food.

v. Sales by State
An interactive map visualizes the geographical distribution of sales across Indian states, allowing users to identify regional differences in revenue.

vi. Top 5 Cities by Sales
A horizontal bar chart ranks the five cities with the highest sales contribution.

vii. Quarterly Performance
A summary table compares quarterly sales, ratings and order volumes, providing an overview of business performance across reporting periods.

viii. Interactive Filters
Three slicers allow users to filter the dashboard by month, food category and restaurant name, making it possible to explore specific segments without modifying the underlying PivotTables.

 5. Key Insights

-> Overall Sales Performance
The dataset records ₹53.01 million in total sales across approximately 197.43 thousand orders, with an average order value of ₹268.51.
The average rating is 4.34 out of 5.

-> Monthly Sales
Monthly sales fluctuate within a relatively narrow range, approximately ₹6.2M–₹6.8M, during the eight months displayed.
Sales decline noticeably from January to February, recover in March and reach another high point in May. Revenue increases again from June through August.

-> Daily Sales
Saturday generates the highest daily sales at ₹7.78M, followed by Thursday at ₹7.66M and Sunday at ₹7.64M.
Tuesday records the lowest daily sales at ₹7.36M.

-> Food Preferences
Vegetarian food contributes 63% of total sales, approximately ₹33.3M, while non-vegetarian food contributes 37%, approximately ₹19.7M.
Vegetarian food therefore accounts for the larger share of revenue in this dataset.

-> Top-Performing Cities
| City      | Total Sales |
| --------- | ----------: |
| Bengaluru |      ₹5.46M |
| Lucknow   |      ₹3.12M |
| Hyderabad |      ₹3.02M |
| Mumbai    |      ₹3.02M |
| New Delhi |      ₹2.83M |

Bengaluru has the highest sales among the five cities displayed, with a substantial lead over the remaining cities. Hyderabad and Mumbai generate nearly identical revenue.

-> Quarterly Performance
| Quarter |  Sales | Rating | Orders |
| ------- | -----: | -----: | -----: |
| Q1      | ₹19.7M |    4.3 |  73.1K |
| Q2      | ₹19.9M |    4.3 |  74.2K |
| Q3      | ₹13.4M |    4.3 |  50.2K |

Q2 records slightly higher sales and order volumes than Q1. Q3 shows lower reported totals, but the dashboard covers only January through August, so Q3 is incomplete and should not be directly compared with full quarters.
The quarterly rating remains approximately 4.3 throughout the reporting period.
